About OCCPL Logistics Pvt. Ltd.

The efficient & flexible logistics company, from Pune to the world.

Meet the OCCPL Logistics Company

At OCCPL Logistics, we have a stellar reputation for providing end-to-end logistics and transport solutions. We are one of the most forward-thinking, efficient logistics service providers in India, delivering services and expanding globally with operations in critical areas around the country.

We recognise that logistics means more than the transfer of materials: it means a high level of competence, expertise and knowledge of processes, local laws and demography. We focus on delivering all that and more.

With 10 offices in Pune district and over 35 offices all over Maharashtra, we strive for fast deliveries and up-to-date online information. We operate all over India, and our customer base has multiplied many times over the last 7 years. With our network throughout India and our own agents in other regions, we help customers from all over the world reach all destinations.

Our Vision

We want to be recognised as the most reliable courier company in the world by empowering lives for our clients, no matter where they are.

Our Mission

We strive to be a result-oriented courier company by creating a reliable logistics network built using advanced technology, which enables us to offer safe and cost-effective courier and logistics solutions to our clients. We are setting up a large-scale integrated logistics division offering warehousing, inventory management, supply chain services and distribution channels.
