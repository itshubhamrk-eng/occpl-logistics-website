Cancellation & Refund Policy

For bookings prepaid to OCCPL Logistics Private Limited.

Cancelling before pickup

You may cancel a prepaid booking before the shipment is picked up. We refund the amount paid, less payment gateway charges.

Cancelling after pickup

Once the shipment has been picked up or dispatched, a cancellation is not refunded.

How to ask for a cancellation or refund

Call or email customer care with your AWB / docket number, the date and amount of the payment, and the reason.

How refunds are paid

An approved refund goes to the original payment method and takes 5 to 7 working days to reach you after approval.

Other payment or service problems

If you were charged twice, a payment failed but money was deducted, or we could not provide the service you paid for, contact customer care so we can check the payment. Nothing in this policy limits your rights under applicable consumer law.
