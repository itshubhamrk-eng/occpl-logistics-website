Pricing

How your courier or cargo charge is worked out.

All prices are in Indian Rupees (INR, ₹). Because every shipment differs, check your rate with our rate calculator. You see the amount payable before you pay.

What your price depends on

  • Chargeable weight: the higher of actual weight and volumetric weight. Work it out with our volumetric weight calculator.
  • Distance: the pickup and delivery pincodes, or the destination country.
  • Service: domestic or international courier and cargo, logistics, or hyperlocal delivery.
  • Taxes: GST as applicable.
